## Overlaps resolved

- Promotion: original `mvpro` (Form 1) and `promo` (Form 2, after acting) kept; M12 used for *Promotion following acting appointment*; `promo` remains available.
- Acting extension: original `ext` and M10 overlap; M10 is the default (states previous and revised end dates), `ext` kept for legacy records.
- FWA approval/refusal: original `fwok`/`fwno` and M1/M2 overlap; M1/M2 are defaults, originals kept.
- Resignation withdrawal: no original letter; M26/M27 added.
- Redundancy notice (`rdn`) kept; its withdrawal uses M25.
- Demotion is routed through the Level 4 dismissal/disciplinary authority row because the matrix lists demotion under Level 4 and has no separate row — **requires Management policy confirmation**.
